TOPIC: glucose_guidelines
DISEASE: general
SOURCE: medical_guidelines
KEYWORDS: glucose, blood sugar, normal, fasting, mg/dL

Healthy blood glucose guidelines:
Normal fasting glucose: 70 to 100 mg/dL.
Normal post meal glucose: below 140 mg/dL.
Prediabetes fasting glucose: 100 to 125 mg/dL.
Diabetes fasting glucose: 126 mg/dL or higher.
Hypoglycemia low blood sugar: below 70 mg/dL — dangerous.
Hyperglycemia high blood sugar: above 180 mg/dL — dangerous.
HbA1c normal: below 5.7%.
HbA1c prediabetes: 5.7 to 6.4%.
HbA1c diabetes: 6.5% or higher.

SOURCE: medical_guidelines
KEYWORDS: BMI, body mass index, underweight, normal, overweight, obese

Healthy BMI ranges:
Underweight: BMI below 18.5 — may indicate malnutrition.
Normal weight: BMI 18.5 to 24.9 — healthy range.
Overweight: BMI 25 to 29.9 — moderate health risk.
Obese Class 1: BMI 30 to 34.9 — high health risk.
Obese Class 2: BMI 35 to 39.9 — very high health risk.
Obese Class 3: BMI 40 and above — extremely high health risk.
BMI above 30 significantly increases diabetes and heart disease risk.

TOPIC: blood_pressure_guidelines
DISEASE: general
SOURCE: medical_guidelines
KEYWORDS: blood pressure, normal, high, hypertension, mmHg, systolic

Healthy blood pressure ranges:
Normal: below 120/80 mmHg — ideal range.
Elevated: 120 to 129 systolic — lifestyle changes needed.
High Stage 1: 130 to 139 systolic — monitor closely.
High Stage 2: 140 or higher systolic — medication may be needed.
Hypertensive crisis: above 180 systolic — seek emergency care.
Low blood pressure hypotension: below 90/60 mmHg.
High blood pressure increases risk of heart disease stroke and kidney disease.

Healthy cholesterol levels:
Total cholesterol normal: below 200 mg/dL.
Total cholesterol borderline: 200 to 239 mg/dL.
Total cholesterol high: 240 mg/dL and above.
LDL bad cholesterol normal: below 100 mg/dL.
LDL borderline: 130 to 159 mg/dL.
LDL high: 160 mg/dL and above.
HDL good cholesterol healthy: above 60 mg/dL.
HDL low risk: below 40 mg/dL for men, below 50 mg/dL for women.
Triglycerides normal: below 150 mg/dL.

TOPIC: diabetes_warning_signs
DISEASE: general
SOURCE: medical_guidelines
KEYWORDS: diabetes symptoms, warning signs, urination, thirst, fatigue

Warning signs of diabetes:
Frequent urination especially at night — polyuria.
Excessive thirst and dry mouth — polydipsia.
Unexplained weight loss despite eating normally.
Extreme fatigue and weakness throughout the day.
Blurred vision that comes and goes.
Slow healing cuts bruises and wounds.
Frequent infections of skin gums or bladder.
Tingling or numbness in hands and feet — neuropathy.
Dark patches of skin around neck and armpits — acanthosis nigricans.
If you experience these symptoms, consult a qualified doctor for evaluation.

TOPIC: heart_warning_signs
DISEASE: general
SOURCE: medical_guidelines
KEYWORDS: heart disease symptoms, warning signs, chest pain, shortness of breath

Warning signs of heart disease:
Chest pain pressure tightness or discomfort.
Shortness of breath during rest or mild activity.
Pain radiating to left arm jaw neck or back.
Dizziness or lightheadedness especially during activity.
Irregular heartbeat or palpitations.
Swelling in legs ankles and feet — edema.
Unusual fatigue with minimal physical effort.
Cold sweats and nausea without obvious cause.
Seek emergency medical help immediately if these symptoms occur.
Do not ignore chest pain — it could be a heart attack.

DISEASE: general
SOURCE: medical_guidelines
KEYWORDS: diabetes prevention, lifestyle, diet, exercise, healthy weight

Diabetes prevention guidelines:
Maintain healthy weight with BMI below 25.
Exercise at least 30 minutes daily — walking cycling or swimming.
Eat balanced diet low in sugar and refined carbohydrates.
Choose whole grains over white rice and white bread.
Eat plenty of vegetables fruits and lean proteins.
Avoid sugary drinks sodas and fruit juices.
Avoid smoking completely — smoking increases diabetes risk by 30 to 40 percent.
Limit alcohol consumption.
Monitor blood glucose regularly especially after age 40.
Get regular medical checkups every 6 to 12 months.

DISEASE: general
SOURCE: medical_guidelines
KEYWORDS: heart disease prevention, lifestyle, diet, exercise, healthy

Heart disease prevention guidelines:
Maintain healthy blood pressure below 120/80 mmHg.
Keep total cholesterol below 200 mg/dL.
Exercise regularly at least 150 minutes per week.
Eat heart healthy foods — fruits vegetables whole grains and fish.
Avoid saturated fats trans fats and processed foods.
Avoid smoking completely — smoking doubles heart disease risk.
Limit alcohol to moderate levels — one drink per day for women two for men.
Manage stress through meditation yoga or relaxation techniques.
Get regular heart checkups especially after age 40.
Maintain healthy weight and BMI below 25.

DISEASE: general
SOURCE: medical_guidelines
KEYWORDS: doctor, emergency, medical help, symptoms, urgent

When to seek medical attention:
See a doctor for confirmatory testing if fasting glucose is 126 mg/dL or higher.
See a doctor if blood pressure consistently above 140/90 mmHg.
See a doctor if BMI is above 35.
Seek emergency care for chest pain lasting more than a few minutes.
Seek emergency care for sudden severe headache or vision changes.
Seek emergency care for sudden numbness or weakness on one side of body.
Get tested for diabetes if you have two or more risk factors.
Get heart checkup if you are male above 45 or female above 55.
Regular screening is recommended for all adults above 40 years.
MediRisk predictions should always be confirmed by a qualified doctor.
